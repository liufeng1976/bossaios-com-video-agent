# BossAI Agent Terms / BossAI Agent 服务条款

> Pre-release legal draft; not yet approved for production customer distribution.
> 预发布法律草案；尚未批准用于正式客户发行。

## Product service / 产品服务

**English.** BossAI Agent is a Windows-first AI video-production desktop product provided in Free Personal, Personal Pro and Business tiers. Actual feature availability, usage quota, subscription status, device eligibility and Business authorization are determined by BossAI's authoritative entitlement infrastructure.

**中文。** BossAI Agent 是 Windows 优先的 AI 视频生产桌面产品，提供 Free Personal、Personal Pro 和 Business 三档。实际功能、使用额度、订阅状态、设备资格及 Business 授权均以 BossAI 权威 entitlement 基础设施为准。

## Free Personal / 个人免费版

**English.** Free Personal is intended for personal use and includes a recurring monthly baseline quota. The exact quota, reset date and available balance must be read from BossAI's authoritative Points/quota service. Unused quota does not automatically carry over unless the applicable plan says otherwise.

**中文。** Free Personal 面向个人用途，包含按月恢复的基础额度。具体额度、恢复日期和剩余量必须从 BossAI 权威 Points/Quota 服务读取。除非适用套餐另有说明，未使用额度不自动结转。

## Personal Pro / 个人专业版

**English.** Personal Pro requires registration and an active subscription. It may provide higher monthly limits, additional features or preferential service levels as listed in the authoritative entitlement record.

**中文。** Personal Pro 需要注册并保持有效订阅，可提供更高月度额度、附加功能或更高服务等级，具体以权威 entitlement 记录为准。

## Business / 商业版

**English.** Business authorization is required for product use that falls within BossAI's applicable business/commercial-use policy, including company production, client deliverables, monetized services, managed deployment, resale, white-label/OEM or similar commercial operations. Business rights, devices, seats, quotas, support and deployment scope are determined by the executed commercial agreement and entitlement.

**中文。** 属于 BossAI 适用商业用途政策的使用，包括公司生产、客户交付、收费/变现服务、集中部署、转售、白标/OEM 或类似商业运营，需要 Business 授权。Business 的权利、设备、席位、额度、支持及部署范围以签署的商业协议和 entitlement 为准。

## Points, quota and BYOK / Points、额度与 BYOK

**English.** AI tasks routed through the BossAI AI Gateway consume BossAI Points according to the authoritative pricing policy. BYOK users pay their own model/provider cost, but BYOK remains subject to product-tier permissions, quota and entitlement. BYOK is not an authorization bypass.

**中文。** 经 BossAI AI Gateway 执行的 AI 任务按权威计价规则扣除 BossAI Points。BYOK 用户自行承担模型/Provider 成本，但 BYOK 仍受产品套餐、额度和 entitlement 约束，不得作为绕过授权的手段。
