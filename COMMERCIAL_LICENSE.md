## Product tiers / 产品分层

| Tier / 套餐 | Intended use / 适用场景 | Authorization / 授权 |
|---|---|---|
| Free Personal | Personal use with recurring baseline quota / 个人用途，按月恢复基础额度 | BossAI account + authoritative Free entitlement / BossAI 账号 + 权威 Free entitlement |
| Personal Pro | Higher personal quota and subscribed features / 更高个人额度及订阅功能 | Active BossAI subscription / 有效 BossAI 订阅 |

## Free Personal / 个人免费版

**English.** Free Personal is permanently available as a product tier, subject to the monthly Points/task quota assigned by BossAI's authoritative commercial infrastructure. The product must not invent or locally mint a second quota ledger.

**中文。** Free Personal 作为产品套餐长期提供，但受 BossAI 权威商业基础设施下发的月度 Points/任务额度约束。产品不得另建或本地伪造第二套额度账本。

## Personal Pro / 个人专业版

**English.** Personal Pro requires registration and an active paid subscription. Subscription status, quota, supported devices and features are read from BossAI entitlement.

**中文。** Personal Pro 需要注册并保持有效付费订阅。订阅状态、额度、支持设备和功能均从 BossAI entitlement 读取。

## Business / 商业版

**English.** Business authorization is the commercial-use tier for organizations and professional production. The applicable agreement may define seats, devices, quotas, managed deployment, support, connectors, SLA, white-label/OEM or other enterprise rights.

**中文。** Business 是面向组织及专业生产的商业用途套餐。适用协议可规定席位、设备、额度、集中部署、支持、连接器、SLA、白标/OEM 或其他企业权利。

## Authority boundary / 权威边界

**English.** License, subscription, Points, quota, device binding and entitlement remain authoritative in BossAI's existing commercial infrastructure. BossAI Agent is a client and local execution product; it must not become a second billing or License authority.

**中文。** License、Subscription、Points、Quota、Device Binding 和 Entitlement 的权威均保留在 BossAI 现有商业基础设施。BossAI Agent 只是客户端和本地执行产品，不得成为第二套计费或 License 权威。

## BossAI AI Gateway and BYOK / BossAI AI Gateway 与 BYOK

**English.** Tasks routed through BossAI AI Gateway consume BossAI Points under the authoritative pricing policy. BYOK users bear external provider/model charges themselves, but BYOK remains subject to plan permissions, quota, device and entitlement restrictions.
