Optional BossAI Gateway, Personal Pro, cross-device or other cloud/governed capabilities may use authoritative plan allowance, Points/quota, device binding and entitlement. Local technical availability never grants commercial-use rights.

**中文。** Free Personal 本地核心面向符合条件的个人及非商业用途长期提供，并受适用仓库/产品条款约束。安装所需本地运行时后，本地核心**无需 BossAI 账号、BossAI Points、月度任务额度或云模型即可本地运行**。可选 BossAI Gateway、Personal Pro、跨设备或其他云端/受治理能力可以使用权威套餐额度、Points/Quota、设备绑定及 entitlement。本地技术可用性绝不自动授予商业使用权。

**English.** BossAI Agent does not create a second License, subscription, Points, quota, device-binding or entitlement authority. Eligible Free Personal local core does not need those commercial records merely to run locally. When Pro/Business/Gateway/cross-device/managed capabilities require commercial records, the authoritative source is BossAI's existing commercial infrastructure; local state may only cache/project it.

**中文。** BossAI Agent 不创建第二套 License、Subscription、Points、Quota、Device Binding 或 Entitlement 权威。符合条件的 Free Personal 本地核心不会因为本地运行而要求这些商业记录；当 Pro/Business/Gateway/跨设备/集中管理能力需要商业记录时，唯一权威来源仍是 BossAI 现有商业基础设施，本地状态只能缓存/投影。

BYOK users bear external provider/model charges themselves; BYOK remains subject to any applicable product-tier, commercial-use, device, quota and entitlement restrictions and does not create commercial rights.

**中文。** 用户明确选择通过 BossAI AI Gateway 路由的任务，可按照权威计价规则消耗 BossAI Points/套餐额度；Gateway 绝不是本地 runtime 失败时的静默 fallback。BYOK 用户自行承担外部 Provider/模型费用，但仍受适用的产品套餐、商业用途、设备、额度和 entitlement 限制，也不会因此获得商业使用权。
